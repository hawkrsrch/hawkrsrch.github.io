### Current Ole Miss Students

If you’re interested in research opportunities with me, please plan to take my courses, such as Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Multimodal Foundation Models. Afterward, feel free to email me your CV, and we can set up a time to discuss potential projects in person.


### Prospective Students

**PhD students:** If you are interested in pursuing a PhD at Ole Miss and would like to work with me, please email your CV and transcripts with the subject line:

`[27Spring (or other semester you plan to start) PhD Application - Your Name]`

For more information about computer vision and deep learning research and its impact, I encourage you to read [my blog post](/blog/2024/research-impact).

Master’s and undergraduate students: Please apply directly through the Department of Computer and Information Science and the university’s standard admissions process.
